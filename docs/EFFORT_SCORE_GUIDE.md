# Exercise Effort Score Guidelines

## Scale: 1-10

### Rating System

- **1-2**: Mobility, stretching, recovery (minimal metabolic demand)
- **3-4**: Light cardio, technique work, warm-up movements
- **5-6**: Moderate intensity, accessory lifts, standard cardio
- **7-8**: Heavy compound lifts, moderate skill movements
- **9**: Olympic lifts, advanced gymnastics, max efforts
- **10**: Most technical/demanding movements (rare)

### Category Averages

- **BODYWEIGHT**: 3-7 (depends on skill level)
- **GYM**: 6-8 (compound > isolation)
- **WEIGHTLIFTING**: 8-9 (high technical demand)
- **CROSSFIT**: 5-8 (varies greatly)
- **CARDIO**: 4-7 (depends on intensity)
- **OTHER**: 5-8 (specialty movements)

## Usage in Training Load Calculation

### Formula for Workout Intensity:

```
Total Workout Load = Σ (EffortScore × Reps × Weight_Factor × Time_Factor)
```

### Weight Factor:

- Bodyweight: 1.0
- Light (<50% 1RM): 1.2
- Moderate (50-75% 1RM): 1.5
- Heavy (75-90% 1RM): 2.0
- Max (>90% 1RM): 2.5

### Time Factor (for time-based):

- <5min: 1.0
- 5-15min: 1.3
- 15-30min: 1.6
- > 30min: 2.0

### Example Calculation:

**Workout: Grace (30 Clean & Jerks at 60kg)**

- EffortScore: 10.0
- Reps: 30
- Weight Factor: 1.5 (moderate weight)
- Time Factor: 1.0 (< 5min typically)
- **Load = 10 × 30 × 1.5 × 1.0 = 450 points**

**Workout: 100 Burpees**

- EffortScore: 6.5
- Reps: 100
- Weight Factor: 1.0 (bodyweight)
- Time Factor: 1.3 (5-15min)
- **Load = 6.5 × 100 × 1.0 × 1.3 = 845 points**

## Adjustments

These values are starting points. You can adjust based on:

- Individual athlete experience
- Volume (high reps lower effort per rep)
- Tempo (slow tempo increases effort)
- Equipment (chains, bands add difficulty)
- Fatigue state (later in workout = higher perceived effort)
